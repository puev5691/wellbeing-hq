## Approved engineering information classes

FACT:
established fact with evidence.

MEASUREMENT:
object/value/unit/method or instrument/conditions/known accuracy or limitation.

CALCULATION:
inputs/units/method or model/assumptions/result/required verification are reproducible.

ASSUMPTION:
explicit assumption, never promoted by repetition.

HYPOTHESIS:
testable proposal with possible evidence/test.

UNKNOWN:
insufficient/conflicting evidence; no memory reconstruction.

VERIFIED_RESULT:
exact result + criterion + actually executed verification + evidence + scope of applicability. Does not imply approval/release.

CANDIDATE/DRAFT:
model/drawing/decision before required verification/approval.
